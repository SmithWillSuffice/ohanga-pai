## Against Scientific Socialism and Historical Materialism

I am dead against materialism of all forms. I employ physicalism when I 
work, because my profession is theoretical physics. But I never believe 
physics (material reality) is _all there is._  It is an oppression to impose 
that ideology upon others ... who are not engaged at the moment in the science of physics!

Lenin of all people said as much.

In the manuscript 
_[What is to be Done?](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/download/what-itd.pdf)_ 
Lenin calls for socialists to recognize the struggle of all, not just 
"the worker" and especially not "just the workers at this workplace".

> The rural superintendents and the flogging of peasants, the corruption of 
the officials and the police treatment of the “common people” in the cities, 
the fight against the famine-stricken and the suppression of the 
popular striving towards enlightenment and knowledge, the extortion of 
taxes and the persecution of the religious sects, the humiliating treatment 
of soldiers and the barrack methods in the treatment of the students and 
liberal intellectuals --- do all these and a thousand other similar 
manifestations of tyranny, though not directly connected with 
the “economic” struggle, represent, in general, less “widely
applicable” means and occasions for political agitation and for drawing 
the masses into the political struggle? The very opposite is true. 

For a genuine socialist, the persecution of one is the persecution 
of all.
